# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need a referral?
No referral is needed to book massage therapy, physiotherapy, acupuncture or counselling at Q's Wellness Clinic.

## How long are sessions?
Sessions run 30 to 60 minutes depending on the service. Your first visit includes an assessment.

## Do you direct bill insurance?
Yes. We offer direct billing to many extended-health insurers. Please bring your insurance details. Coverage depends on your plan; the front desk can confirm.

## Do you accept ICBC claims?
Yes. ICBC claims are accepted. We will need your claim number and date of accident.
