# ICBC post-injury rehab

ICBC is BC's motor-vehicle insurer. If you were injured in a car accident in BC you may be eligible for treatment covered through your ICBC claim.

- We accept ICBC claims.
- To get started, front-desk staff need: your claim number (if you have one), the date of the accident, and the best phone number to reach you.
- If you have not opened a claim yet, report the accident to ICBC. Staff can explain next steps.
- ICBC patients usually start with a physiotherapy assessment.
- Treatment coverage and approvals are decided by ICBC, not by the clinic. The AI receptionist collects intake details and creates a ticket for the front desk to follow up.
