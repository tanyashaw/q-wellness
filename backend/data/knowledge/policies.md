# Clinic policies

## Cancellation policy (demo placeholder)
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged a fee. The front desk can discuss exceptions.

## Call recording
Calls with the virtual receptionist are recorded to help us serve you. Callers are told at the start of the call.

## Privacy
Q's Wellness Clinic handles personal information under BC's PIPA. The AI receptionist never gives medical advice and never shares health details in text messages.

## Messaging consent
Marketing messages are sent only with consent (CASL). Every message includes "Reply STOP to opt out".

## Emergencies
For a medical emergency, call 911. The clinic is not an emergency service.
