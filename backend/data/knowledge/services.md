# Services at Q's Wellness Clinic

Q's Wellness Clinic is a multi-discipline therapy clinic in Surrey, BC, open since 2018.

## Registered Massage Therapy (RMT)
Remedial massage, sports massage, head massage, and prenatal / maternity massage. Sessions are 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Our RMTs include Khalid Qasmi and Shaniya.

## Physiotherapy
Assessment-led physiotherapy with Anjanjeet Shergill. The first visit is an initial assessment; follow-ups are shorter.

## Acupuncture
Acupuncture sessions with a registered acupuncturist.

## Clinical Counselling
Clinical counselling sessions, 50 minutes.

## ICBC Post-Injury Rehab
Rehabilitation after a motor-vehicle accident for patients with an ICBC claim. See the ICBC document.

## Conditions we commonly treat
Ankle sprain, carpal tunnel, disc herniation, frozen shoulder, headaches and migraines, knee pain / ITB, Maigne syndrome, neck pain, patellofemoral pain syndrome (PFPS), tennis elbow, thoracic outlet syndrome.
We do not diagnose over the phone. A practitioner assesses you at your first visit.
